# Site audit: SEO, AEO and GEO, 18 September 2026

Where camzify.com stands against the three disciplines defined in
[`PLAYBOOK.md`](PLAYBOOK.md), what the data says about the US, Singapore and UAE
markets, which competitors win and why, what was fixed in this pass, and the ordered
backlog. Every number here came from a tool call or a script run on this date; nothing
is estimated. Re-run the scripts and the DataForSEO pulls before trusting any figure
older than a month.

Data sources: DataForSEO Labs (ranked keywords, keyword overview, SERP, backlinks,
Lighthouse), DataForSEO AI Optimization (18 live LLM responses), `scripts/seo-audit.py`
over 215 rendered routes, `scripts/check-pages.py`.

---

## 1. Verdict

**Structurally the site is ahead of every competitor page the audit looked at.** All
215 routes have one H1, a canonical, breadcrumbs, FAQ schema where content warrants it,
a single JSON-LD graph, an explicit AI-crawler allowlist and an `llms.txt`. Lighthouse
gives the homepage SEO 1.0 and best practices 1.0. Average audit scores after this pass: SEO 99,
AEO 91, GEO 99 out of 100 (SEO 97, AEO 88, GEO 97 before it).

**Commercially it is starting from zero.** The domain ranks for 15 keywords, all through
two old blog URLs that 404ed since the rebuild (now redirected), has 39 backlinks all to
the homepage, and is cited by AI engines for exactly one topic, virtual patrolling. The
gap is not page quality; it is age, links and the absence of the specific quotable
sentences engines lift. This pass closed the sentence gap on the highest-value queries.
Links and age cannot be coded.

**Can the content surpass competitors?** On depth and honesty, yes: no competitor page
in the pulls carries sourced comparisons, stated limits and a per-camera floor price
together. On the two things that decide rank in the first year, links and freshness
signals, the competitors are years ahead. The backlog is ordered to earn both.

| video analytics | not pulled | 30 | 140 |

Findings:

- **The US is the market.** Every phrase is 10 to 50 times larger there. The site's
  US-English, US-first positioning is right; Singapore and the UAE rank on the same
  pages.
- **Difficulty is low on the phrases the site already has pages for:** "cloud based
  video surveillance" KD 3, "business security camera system" KD 2, "retail security
  cameras" KD 1, "warehouse security cameras" KD 7, "ai security camera" KD 7,
  "apartment security" KD 6, "remote video monitoring" KD 1, "cloud nvr" KD 1. These
  need links, not new content.
- **Singapore and the UAE use "CCTV".** Related searches in both markets were "cctv
  cloud storage", "cloud cctv", "cctv cloud storage price". The SERPs there are the
  global vendors (Genetec, Avigilon, Milestone, Axis) plus videoloft and camcloud; no
  local specialist holds the page. A well-linked English page with CCTV vocabulary can
  take those positions quickly because the volumes are small and the competition is
  generic.
- **"virtual patrolling" has no reportable volume anywhere.** The site ranks for it
  and is cited for it because almost nobody else targets the phrase. It is a
  differentiator, not a traffic source; the traffic phrases are "virtual guard" (480 US,
  57 AI searches a month) and "remote video monitoring".
- **AI search volume** (how often people type the phrase into an LLM): "virtual guard"
  57, "security camera software" 57, "video management system" 42, "cloud cctv" 41,
  "what is a cloud vms" 32, "best video management software" 29, "cloud vms cost" 21,
  "cloud nvr" 15. These are the queries to defend in `05-ai-visibility.csv`.

## 5. What was changed in this pass (18 September 2026)

Committed to `development`; merge to `main` to publish.

1. **Quotable facts added where engines look for them.** `/pricing` FAQ "How much does
   Camzify cost?" (the $5 floor, the three billing units, no hardware).
   `/supported-cameras` FAQ "Does Camzify work with ONVIF and RTSP cameras?" (the one
   sentence een.com is cited for).
2. **Glossary cross-linking.** Each term now links six related terms chosen by mention
   (which terms it names, which name it), so every glossary page has inbound links
   from more than the hub.
3. **Buyer's guide links every one-to-one comparison**, lifting six comparison pages
   from two inbound links to three or more.
4. **Bylines with a visible "Updated" date** on every guide (`AuthorByline updated=`),
   plus author and article schema on the six how-to guides that lacked them.
   Perplexity favors dated pages.
5. **Eight thinnest pages expanded** to 900 to 1,400 words with definition-first ledes
   and question headings: four category comparisons and four guides (see the README
   entry for the list and word counts).
6. **`areaServed` names the United Arab Emirates** explicitly in the Organization
   schema, alongside the United States and Singapore.
7. **New audit tooling**: `scripts/seo-audit.py` (rendered crawl with SEO/AEO/GEO
   scores per route), `04-site-audit.csv`, `05-ai-visibility.csv` and `.html`, this
   report and the playbook.

Earlier the same day: the old blog redirects, the VMS guide, the alternatives silo and
the best-cloud-VMS guide (README, "Ranking check" and "Alternative pages" entries).

## 6. Backlog, in order

Each item: the change, the evidence, and what it needs. Items 1 to 3 are outside the
codebase and matter more than anything in it.

1. **Links.** 36 referring domains, all to the homepage, is the binding constraint on
   every phrase above. Targets that fit the brief: security-industry directories and
   associations (US), the Singapore and UAE integrator and installer directories,
   partner pages of the camera brands listed on `/supported-cameras`, and one
   published piece per quarter that cites the guides. Needs: the business's outreach
   time. No code.
2. **Google Business Profile for the Singapore HQ**, matching `lib/site-config.ts`
   exactly. The strongest local entity signal available. Needs: the business.
3. **Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools** connected, sitemap submitted, the
   redirected blog URLs requested for re-crawl. Needs: the business's Google account.
4. **AI detection catalog.** One table on `/ai-features`: detection, one-line
   definition, typical use, licensed or included. The unit ChatGPT quoted from Axis
   documentation. Code only; two hours.
5. **Virtual guard vs on-site guard table** on `/virtual-guard`, matching the format
   Perplexity and ChatGPT cited (presence, coverage, response, cost basis, best for).
   Code only, no figures.
6. **Definition-first ledes on the 78 flagged pages.** The heuristic over-flags
   (industry and use-case pages open with the buyer's situation, which is right), so
   review by silo: guides and comparisons first, where the definition should be
   sentence one. Two to three sentences per page.
7. **CCTV vocabulary for Singapore and the UAE.** Add "cloud CCTV" and "CCTV cloud
   storage" as secondary phrases on `/cloud-video-surveillance`,
   `/platform/video-backup-and-retention` and two glossary entries ("cloud CCTV" as a
   new term). Code only.
8. **Regional pages** (`/singapore`, `/uae`) once the business supplies: where video is
   stored for that market, support hours and language, local contact, currency, any
   local partner. Without those facts, do not build them.
9. **Remote video monitoring cost.** Engines cite pages with per-camera ranges. Decide
   whether to publish a range for the monitoring use case or hold the floor-only
   policy. If a range is published it must be a real quoted range from the business,
   stated as such.
10. **Homepage weight.** 2.7 MB total; the hero image ladder is the cost. Serve the
    smallest variant first on mobile and defer the below-fold camera wall. Performance
    0.89 to 0.95 is realistic.
11. **Accessibility 0.83.** Run Lighthouse with full audit output to get the failing
    items (likely contrast on muted text and missing names on icon buttons); fix in
    the design tokens, not per page.
12. **Thin utility and hub pages.** `/compare` (288 words), `/industries` (263),
    `/partners` (342), `/guides` (513): each hub should carry a 150-word framing
    paragraph per group and a definition of the category, so the hub itself can rank
    for the category phrase.
13. **Re-check the ADT alternative page from a US connection** and replace the archive
    capture dates with a live read.
14. **Monthly loop** per the playbook: ranked keywords, audit script, 18 live LLM
    queries, Search Console. First run: 18 October 2026.
