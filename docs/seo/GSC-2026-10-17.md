## The one real defect

`robots.txt` disallowed `/_next/`, which holds every stylesheet and script. Googlebot
renders pages with them, so it was seeing the site unstyled, and it reported the CSS files
as blocked and even as indexed. Fixed in `app/robots.ts`.

## Recommendations, in order

1. **Turn on measurement first.** Enable Web Analytics in the Vercel project (Analytics tab).
   Custom events such as "Lead" need a Pro plan; check whether GA4 is receiving
   `generate_lead` meanwhile. Without this, no recommendation below can be judged by demos.
2. **Match the closing call to the page.** Every page ends with "Ready to patrol your site
   24/7?", but the pages that draw search traffic are about cloud VMS, analytics, pricing and
   camera setup. A closing band per section (cloud VMS: see your own cameras in the cloud;
   camera setup: connect this camera; pricing: get a quote for your camera count) through
   PageShell's existing `ctaProps`.
3. **Win page 1 for the head terms.** Cloud VMS, video analytics, virtual patrol and cloud
   video surveillance carry 5,500 impressions a month at positions 18 to 23. Two levers:
   - **One page per term.** "cloud vms" is answered by /guides/what-is-a-cloud-vms,
     /glossary/cloud-vms, /cloud-video-surveillance and /guides/best-cloud-vms at once.
     Pick the page that should rank for each term and point internal anchors at it.
   - **Links from outside.** Position on head terms is mostly authority. Partner pages
     (Nettbox), integrator and reseller listings, software directories (G2, Capterra,
     SourceForge), and security trade press, US first.
4. **Lean into pricing intent.** Pricing queries rank at 8.6, the closest to page 1 of any
   theme, and are the closest to a demo. Keep titles and descriptions on pricing pages
   carrying the public figures; consider a short "what a quote includes" section on /pricing.
5. **Convert the camera-setup visitors.** Setup guides (RTMP, EZVIZ, Reolink) get the most
   clicks per impression, but their readers are often installers or owners fixing one camera
   (the Blue Iris contact form was one). Give those pages a closing call about connecting
   the camera to cloud recording and alerts, not patrols.
6. **Alternatives pages where people already look.** "eagle eye alternatives" (71
   impressions, position 15) and "avigilon alternatives" (position 3.9) show demand; an
   /alternatives/eagle-eye page would follow the Verkada and Blue Iris pattern.
7. **Blog topics from this data, one at a time.** Candidates with impressions already:
   "what is cctv" (103, position 11.4), "camera health monitoring" (66, 13.9), "security
   patrolling report" (49, 8.9), "remote video response" (29, 5.8). Each with keyword
   research first, as agreed.
8. **Re-check on 31 October** against this file.
